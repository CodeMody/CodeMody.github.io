#About

I like building things end to end — and I like them to move. Some of my projects bounce: arcade games made in Godot and Pygame. Some blink: MicroPython firmware on small boards like the Calliope mini, wired up over Bluetooth LE.

And when my daily tools annoy me, I fix them myself — that's how my DeepSeek Harness and OpenCode plugin bundles were born. Everything lives on GitHub, one honest commit message at a time.

Games

Juice-heavy breakouts and slingshot physics — playable, seeded, and procedural.

Embedded

MicroPython on the Calliope mini — sensors, BLE streams, and firmware with tests.

Dev Tools

Plugins that tame context compaction, MCP servers, and background services.

DigiBall

A tough, child-safe ball with a Calliope mini at its core. It recognises throws, catches and flight times, and streams the game state to a phone app over Bluetooth LE — kids play short mini-games against each other. “A ball that plays along.”
